26  Second Gambit

game
With four back-games; two from the 4th, one from the 9th, and the last from the 11th move.

1.White. The king's pawn, two moves.Black. The same.
2.White. The king's bishop's pawn, two moves.Black. The pawn takes the pawn.
3.White. The king's bishop, at his queen's bishop's fourth square.Black. The queen gives check.
4.White. The king, at his bishop's square.Black. The king's knight's pawn, two moves. (a)
5.White. The king's knight, at his bishop's third square.Black. The queen, at her king's rook's fourth square. (b)
6.White. The queen's pawn, two moves.Black. The queen's pawn, one move.
7.White. The queen's bishop's pawn, one move. (c)Black. The queen's bishop, at the adverse king's knight's fourth square.
8.White. The king, at his bishop's second square.Black. The king's knight, at his king's bishop's third square. (d)
9.White. The queen, at her king's second square.Black. The queen's knight, at his queen's second square.
10.White. The king's rook's pawn, two moves.Black. The bishop takes the knight.
11.White. The queen takes the bishop.Black. The queen takes the queen. (e)
12.White. The king retakes the queen. (f)Black. The king's knight's pawn gives check.
13.White. The king takes the adverse king's pawn.Black. The king's bishop gives check at the rook's third square.
14.White. The king, at the adverse king's bishop's fourth square. (g)Black. The king's bishop takes the adverse queen's bishop.
15.White. The king's rook takes the bishop.Black. The king's rook's pawn, two moves.
16.White. The knight, at his queen's second square.Black. The king, at his second square.
17.White. The king's rook, at its king's bishop's square. (h)Black. The queen's bishop's pawn, one move. (i)
18.White. The queen's rook, at its king's square.Black. The queen's knight's pawn, two moves. (k)
19.White. The bishop, at his queen's knight's third square.Black. The queen's rook's pawn, two moves.
20.White. The king's pawn, one move.Black. The pawn takes the pawn.
21.White. The queen's pawn retakes the pawn.Black. The king's knight, at his queen's fourth square.
22.White. The knight, at his king's fourth square. (l)Black. The queen's knight, at his third square.
23.White. The knight, at the adverse king's bishop's third square.Black. The queen's rook, at its queen's square. (m)
24.White. The king's pawn, one move.Black. The queen's rook, at its queen's third square. (n)
25.White. The pawn takes the pawn, and gives check with the rook.Black. The king takes the pawn.
26.White. The king, at the adverse king's knight's fourth square.Black. The king, at his knight's second square, to avoid a check by discovery.
27.White. The knight takes the king's rook's pawn, and gives check.Black. The king, at his rook's second square.
28.White. The king's rook gives check.Black. The king, at his knight's square.
29.White. The king's rook, at the adverse queen's knight's second square.Black. The queen's rook, at its queen's square. (o)
30.White. The rook takes the adverse queen's knight, and wins the game in course.
