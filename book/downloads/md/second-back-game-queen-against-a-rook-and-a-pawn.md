# Second Back-game (Queen against a Rook and a Pawn)

*On the 15th move of the Black.*

15. **White.** The queen, at her fourth square.
   **Black.** The king, at his queen's bishop's second square.

16. **White.** The queen, at her rook's fourth square.
   **Black.** The rook, at its queen's bishop's fourth square.

17. **White.** The queen gives check, at the adverse queen's rook's second square.
   **Black.** The king, at his king's bishop's third square.

18. **White.** The king, at the adverse king's second square, and must win.

   > As your king may go backwards, you must needs win the game.


---

*A back-game: a variation resuming its parent game at move 15.*

Source: Philidor, *Analysis of the Game of Chess* (London, P. Elmsley, 1777), collated with volume I of the edition of 1790.
