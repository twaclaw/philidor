# Fourth Back-game of the First Gambit

*From the 6th move of the Black.*

6. **White.** The queen's pawn, two moves.
   **Black.** The queen's bishop's pawn, one move.

7. **White.** The king's pawn, one move.
   **Black.** The queen's knight's pawn, two moves.

8. **White.** The bishop, at his queen's knight's third square.
   **Black.** The queen's rook's pawn, two moves.

9. **White.** The queen's rook's pawn, two moves.
   **Black.** The queen's knight's pawn, one move.

10. **White.** The queen's knight, at his queen's second square.
   **Black.** The queen's bishop, at his rook's third square.

11. **White.** The queen's knight, at his king's fourth square.
   **Black.** The queen, at her knight's third square, or wherever he pleases: he loses the game.

12. **White.** The knight gives check, at the adverse queen's third square.


---

*A back-game: a variation resuming its parent game at move 6.*

Source: Philidor, *Analysis of the Game of Chess* (London, P. Elmsley, 1777), collated with volume I of the edition of 1790.
