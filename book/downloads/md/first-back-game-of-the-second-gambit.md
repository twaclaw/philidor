# First Back-game of the Second Gambit

*From the 4th move of the Black.*

4. **White.** The king, at his bishop's square.
   **Black.** The king's bishop, at his queen's bishop's fourth square.

5. **White.** The queen's pawn, two moves.
   **Black.** The king's bishop, at his queen's knight's third square.

6. **White.** The king's knight, at his bishop's third square.
   **Black.** The queen, at the adverse king's knight's fourth square.

7. **White.** The king's bishop takes the king's bishop's pawn, and gives check.
   **Black.** The king, at his bishop's square, because, if he retakes, he loses his queen.

8. **White.** The king's rook's pawn, one move.
   **Black.** The queen, at the adverse king's knight's third square.

9. **White.** The queen's knight, at his bishop's third square.
   **Black.** The king takes the bishop.

10. **White.** The queen's knight, at his king's second square.
   **Black.** The queen, at her king's knight's third square, having no other place.

11. **White.** The king's knight gives check to the king and queen, and wins the game in course.


---

*A back-game: a variation resuming its parent game at move 4.*

Source: Philidor, *Analysis of the Game of Chess* (London, P. Elmsley, 1777), collated with volume I of the edition of 1790.
