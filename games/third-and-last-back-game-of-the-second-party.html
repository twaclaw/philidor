10  Third and Last Back-game of the Second Party

back-game · branches from Second Party at move 26
Beginning at the 26th move of the Black.

1.White. The king's pawn, two moves.Black. The same.
2.White. The king's bishop, at his queen's bishop's fourth square.Black. The queen's bishop's pawn, one move.
3.White. The queen's pawn, two moves. (a)Black. The pawn takes the pawn. (b)
4.White. The queen retakes the pawn.Black. The queen's pawn, one move.
5.White. The king's bishop's pawn, two moves.Black. The queen's bishop, at his king's third square. (c)
6.White. The king's bishop, at his queen's third square.Black. The queen's pawn, one move.
7.White. The king's pawn, one move.Black. The queen's bishop's pawn, one move.
8.White. The queen, at her king's bishop's second square.Black. The queen's knight, at his queen's bishop's third square. (d)
9.White. The queen's bishop's pawn, one move.Black. The king's knight's pawn, one move.
10.White. The king's rook's pawn, one move.Black. The king's rook's pawn, two moves. (e)
11.White. The king's knight's pawn, one move. (f)Black. The king's knight, at his rook's third square.
12.White. the king's knight, at his bishop's third square.Black. The king's bishop, at his king's second square.
13.White. The queen's rook's pawn, two moves.Black. The king's knight, at his bishop's fourth square.
14.White. The king, at his bishop's square.Black. The king's rook's pawn, one move.
15.White. The king's knight's pawn, one move.Black. The knight gives check to the king, and to the rook.
16.White. The king, at his knight's second square.Black. The knight takes the rook.
17.White. The king takes the knight. (g)Black. The queen, at her second square.
18.White. The queen, at her king's knight's square. (h)Black. The queen's rook's pawn, two moves.
19.White. The queen's bishop, at his king's third square. (i)Black. The queen's knight's pawn, one move.
20.White. The queen's knight, at his rook's third square.Black. The king castles on his queen's side, (k)
21.White. The king's bishop gives check.Black. The king, at his queen's bishop's second square.
22.White. The queen's knight, at his queen's bishop's second square. (l)Black. The queen's rook, at her own home.
23.White. The king's bishop, at the adversary's queen's knight's fourth square.Black. The queen, at her own home, (m)
24.White. The queen's knight's pawn, two moves.Black. The queen, at her king's bishop's square.
25.White. The queen's knight's pawn takes the adverse queen's bishop's pawn.Black. The queen's knight's pawn takes the pawn again.
26.White. The king's knight, at his queen's second square.Black. The king's bishop's pawn, one move.
27.White. The king's knight, at his queen's knight's third square.Black. The queen's bishop's pawn, one move.
28.White. The queen's bishop gives check.Black. The king, at his queen's knight's second square.
29.White. The king's knight gives check, at the black queen's bishop's fourth square.Black. The king's bishop takes the knight.
30.White. The queen's bishop takes the bishop.Black. The queen, at her bishop's square.
31.White. The rook, at its queen's knight's square.Black. The king, at his queen's bishop's second square.
32.White. The queen's bishop gives check at the adversary's queen's third square.Black. The king, at his queen's square.
33.White. The queen gives check, at the adversary's queen's knight's third square.Black. The king, at his home, or any where; loses the game.
