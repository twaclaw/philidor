# A Mate with a single Rook

**Situation of the White**
- The king, at his fourth square.
- The rook, at its king's rook's square.
**Situation of the Black**
- The king, at his third square.

1. **White.** The rook gives check.
   **Black.** The king, at his second square.

2. **White.** The king, at the adverse king's fourth square.
   **Black.** The king, at his queen's second square.

   > His king must be opposite to yours, in order that you may give check with your rook; because then his king is forced, by the check, to retrograde.

3. **White.** The rook, at the adverse king's knight's third square.

   > If you had played your king opposite to his, he would have restored his king to the same place; whereas at this move he plays his king to the face of yours: you may then force him back by the check of your rook.

   **Black.** The king, at his queen's bishop's second square.

4. **White.** The king, at the adverse queen's fourth square.
   **Black.** The king, at his queen's knight's second square.

5. **White.** The king, at the adverse queen's bishop's fourth square.
   **Black.** The king, at his queen's rook's second square.

6. **White.** The king, at the adverse queen's knight's fourth square.

   > In the present case he is obliged to place his king opposite to yours, or absolutely to abandon the line.

   **Black.** The king, at his queen's knight's second square.

7. **White.** The rook gives check, at the adverse king's knight's second square.
   **Black.** The king, at his queen's bishop's square.

8. **White.** The king, at the adverse queen's bishop's third square.
   **Black.** The king, at his queen's square.

9. **White.** The rook, at the adverse queen's rook's second square.
   **Black.** The king, at his home.

10. **White.** The king, at the adverse queen's third square.
   **Black.** The king, at his bishop's square.

11. **White.** The king, at the adverse king's third square.
   **Black.** The king, at his knight's square.

12. **White.** The king, at the adverse king's bishop's third square.
   **Black.** The king, at his rook's square.

13. **White.** The king, at the adverse king's knight's third square.
   **Black.** The king, at his knight's square.

14. **White.** The rook gives check-mate. A GAME WON WITH A QUEEN AGAINST A ROOK AND A PAWN.
