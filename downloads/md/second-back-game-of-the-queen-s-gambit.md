# Second Back-game of the Queen's Gambit

*At the 3d move of the Black.*

3. **White.** The king's pawn, two moves.
   **Black.** The queen's knight's pawn, two moves.

4. **White.** The queen's rook's pawn, two moves.
   **Black.** The queen's bishop's pawn, one move.

5. **White.** The queen's knight's pawn, one move.
   **Black.** The gambit's pawn takes the pawn.

6. **White.** The rook's pawn takes the pawn.
   **Black.** The queen's bishop's pawn retakes the pawn.

7. **White.** The king's bishop takes the pawn, and gives check.
   **Black.** The bishop covers the check.

8. **White.** The queen takes the pawn.
   **Black.** The bishop takes the bishop.

9. **White.** The queen retakes the bishop, and gives check.
   **Black.** The queen covers the check.

10. **White.** The queen takes the queen.
   **Black.** The knight retakes the queen.

11. **White.** The king's bishop's pawn, two moves.
   **Black.** The king's pawn, one move.

12. **White.** The king, at his second square.
   **Black.** The king's bishop's pawn, two moves.

13. **White.** The king's pawn, one move.
   **Black.** The king's knight, at his king's second square.

14. **White.** The queen's knight, at his bishop's third square.
   **Black.** The king's knight, at his queen's fourth square.

15. **White.** The knight takes the knight.
   **Black.** The pawn retakes the knight.

16. **White.** The queen's bishop, at his rook's third square.
   **Black.** The bishop takes the bishop.

17. **White.** The rook takes the bishop.
   **Black.** The king, at his second square.

18. **White.** The king, at his bishop's third square.
   **Black.** The king's rook, at its queen's knight's square.

19. **White.** The knight, at his king's second square.
   **Black.** The king, at his third square.

20. **White.** The king's rook, at its queen's rook's square.
   **Black.** The king's rook, at its queen's knight's second square.

21. **White.** The queen's rook gives check.
   **Black.** The knight covers the check.

22. **White.** The king's rook, at the adverse queen's rook's fourth square.
   **Black.** The king's knight's pawn, one move.

23. **White.** The knight, at his queen's bishop's third square.
   **Black.** The queen's rook, at its queen's square.

24. **White.** The queen's rook takes the adverse queen's rook's pawn.
   **Black.** The rook takes the rook.

25. **White.** The rook retakes, and must win the game afterwards, having the superiority of a pawn.


---

*A back-game: a variation resuming its parent game at move 3.*

Source: Philidor, *Analysis of the Game of Chess* (London, P. Elmsley, 1777), collated with volume I of the edition of 1790.
