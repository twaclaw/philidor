# Fifth Back-game of the Method of Playing

*At the 6th move of the White.*

6. **White.** The queen's pawn, two moves.
   **Black.** The king's pawn takes the pawn.

7. **White.** The queen's bishop's pawn retakes the pawn.
   **Black.** The king's bishop gives check.

8. **White.** The queen's bishop covers the check.
   **Black.** The bishop takes the bishop.

9. **White.** The queen's knight retakes the bishop.
   **Black.** The queen's pawn, one move,

   > This party is entirely equal on both fides: your pawns in the centre are broken. Nevertheless you may venture this fort of play with an antagonist to whom you give some advantage.


---

*A back-game: a variation resuming its parent game at move 6.*

Source: Philidor, *Analysis of the Game of Chess* (London, P. Elmsley, 1777), collated with volume I of the edition of 1790.
