# Third Back-game of the Queen's Gambit

*At the 4th move of the White.*

4. **White.** The queen's pawn takes the pawn.
   **Black.** The queen takes the queen.

5. **White.** The king retakes the queen.
   **Black.** The queen's bishop, at his king's third square.

6. **White.** The king's bishop's pawn, two moves.
   **Black.** The king's knight's pawn, one move.

7. **White.** The queen's knight, at his bishop's third square.
   **Black.** The queen's knight, at his queen's second square.

8. **White.** The king's rook's pawn, one move.
   **Black.** The king's rook's pawn, two moves.

9. **White.** The queen's bishop, at his king's third square.

   **Black.** The pawn retakes the knight.
