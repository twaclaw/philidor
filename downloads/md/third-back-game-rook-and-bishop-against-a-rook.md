# Third Back-game (Rook and Bishop against a Rook)

*On the 7th move of the Black.*

7. **White.** The rook, at its queen's bishop's fourth square.
   **Black.** The king, at his bishop's square.

8. **White.** The bishop, at the adverse king's fourth square.
   **Black.** The king, at his knight's square.

9. **White.** The rook, at its king's rook's fourth square, and must win.


---

*A back-game: a variation resuming its parent game at move 7.*

Source: Philidor, *Analysis of the Game of Chess* (London, P. Elmsley, 1777), collated with volume I of the edition of 1790.
