# Third Back-game of the Third Party

*Beginning from the 10th move of the Black.*

10. **Black.** The king castles on his queen's side.
   **White.** The king castles on his rook's side.

11. **Black.** The king's rook's pawn, one move.
   **White.** The queen's knight, at his queen's second square.

12. **Black.** The king's knight's pawn, two moves.
   **White.** The queen's bishop, at his king's third square.

13. **Black.** The queen's rook, at its king's knight's square.
   **White.** The queen's knight's pawn, two moves.

14. **Black.** The king's rook's pawn, one move.
   **White.** The queen's rook's pawn, two moves.

15. **Black.** The bishop takes the knight.
   **White.** The queen retakes the bishop.

16. **Black.** The king's knight's pawn, one move.
   **White.** The queen, at her king's second square.

17. **Black.** The queen's bishop's pawn, one move.
   **White.** The queen's rook's pawn, one move.

18. **Black.** The bishop, at his queen's bishop's second square.
   **White.** The queen's bishop's pawn, one move.

19. **Black.** The king's rook's pawn, one move.
   **White.** The king's rook, at its queen's knight's square.

20. **Black.** The king's rook, at its fourth square.
   **White.** The queen's bishop's pawn, one move.

21. **Black.** The queen's pawn, one move.
   **White.** The king's pawn, one move.

22. **Black.** The king's knight, at his king's square.
   **White.** The queen's knight's pawn, one move.

23. **Black.** The pawn takes the pawn.
   **White.** The queen's rook retakes the pawn.

24. **Black.** The queen's rook's pawn, one move.
   **White.** The queen's rook, at its knight's fourth square.

25. **Black.** The king's bishop's pawn, one move.
   **White.** The king's bishop takes the queen's rook's pawn.

26. **Black.** The pawn retakes the bishop.
   **White.** The queen retakes the pawn, and gives check.

27. **Black.** The king retires.
   **White.** The queen gives check.

28. **Black.** The knight covers the check.
   **White.** The queen's rook's pawn, one move.

29. **Black.** The king, at his queen's second square.
   **White.** The queen takes the adverse queen's pawn, and gives check.

30. **Black.** The king retires.
   **White.** The queen's rook's pawn, one move; and, by different visible ways, wins the game, without going further.

*A back-game: a variation resuming its parent game at move 10.*
