# Third and Last Back-game of the Second Party

*Beginning at the 26th move of the Black.*

26. **White.** The king's knight, at his queen's second square.
   **Black.** The king's bishop's pawn, one move.

27. **White.** The king's knight, at his queen's knight's third square.
   **Black.** The queen's bishop's pawn, one move.

28. **White.** The queen's bishop gives check.
   **Black.** The king, at his queen's knight's second square.

29. **White.** The king's knight gives check, at the black queen's bishop's fourth square.
   **Black.** The king's bishop takes the knight.

30. **White.** The queen's bishop takes the bishop.
   **Black.** The queen, at her bishop's square.

31. **White.** The rook, at its queen's knight's square.
   **Black.** The king, at his queen's bishop's second square.

32. **White.** The queen's bishop gives check at the adversary's queen's third square.
   **Black.** The king, at his queen's square.

33. **White.** The queen gives check, at the adversary's queen's knight's third square.
   **Black.** The king, at his home, or any where; loses the game.

*A back-game: a variation resuming its parent game at move 26.*

Source: Philidor, *Analysis of the Game of Chess* (London, P. Elmsley, 1777), collated with volume I of the edition of 1790.
