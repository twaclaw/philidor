# Second Back-game of the Third Party

*Beginning at the 5th move of the Black.*

5. **Black.** The king castles.
   **White.** The king's bishop's pawn, one move.

6. **Black.** The queen's pawn, one move.
   **White.** The queen, at her king's bishop's third square.

7. **Black.** The queen's pawn takes the pawn.
   **White.** The queen's pawn retakes the pawn.

8. **Black.** The queen's rook's pawn, two moves.
   **White.** The king's knight's pawn, two moves.

9. **Black.** The queen, at her third square.
   **White.** The king's knight's pawn, one move.

10. **Black.** The king's knight, at his king's square.
   **White.** The king's bishop, at his queen's bishop's fourth square.

11. **Black.** The queen's bishop's pawn, one move.
   **White.** The queen, at the adverse king's rook's fourth square.

12. **Black.** The queen's knight's pawn, two steps.
   **White.** The king's knight's pawn, one step.

13. **Black.** The king's rook's pawn, one move.
   **White.** The bishop takes the adverse king's bishop's pawn.

14. **Black.** The king, at his rook's square.
   **White.** The queen's bishop takes the adverse king's rook's pawn.

15. **Black.** The king's knight, at his bishop's third square.
   **White.** The queen, at her king's rook's fourth square, and afterwards wins the game.


---

*A back-game: a variation resuming its parent game at move 5.*

Source: Philidor, *Analysis of the Game of Chess* (London, P. Elmsley, 1777), collated with volume I of the edition of 1790.
