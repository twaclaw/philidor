# First Back-game of Cunningham's Gambit

*On the 7th move of the Black.*

7. **White.** The king, at his rook's square.
   **Black.** The bishop, at his king's second square.

8. **White.** The king's bishop takes the pawn, and gives check.
   **Black.** The king takes the bishop.

9. **White.** The king's knight, at the adverse king's fourth square, giving double check.
   **Black.** The king, at his third square; any where else, he loses his queen. I iv

10. **White.** The queen gives check, at her king's knight's fourth square.
   **Black.** The king takes the knight.

11. **White.** The queen gives check, at the adverse king's bishop's fourth square.
   **Black.** The king, at his queen's third square.

12. **White.** The queen gives check-mate at the adverse queen's fourth square.

*A back-game: a variation resuming its parent game at move 7.*
