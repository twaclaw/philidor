3. **White.** The queen's bishop's pawn, one move.

4. **White.** The queen, at her king's second square.

5. **White.** The queen's pawn, one move.
   **Black.** The king's knight, at the adverse king's knight's fourth square.

   **Black.** The queen, at her king's bishop's third square,

   **Black.** The king's knight, at the adverse king's third square.
