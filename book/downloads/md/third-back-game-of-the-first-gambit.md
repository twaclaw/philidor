# Third Back-game of the First Gambit

*On the 5th move of the Black.*

5. **White.** The king's rook's pawn, two moves.
   **Black.** The king's knight's pawn, one move.

6. **White.** The king's knight, at the adverse king's knight's fourth square.
   **Black.** The king's knight, at his rook's third square.

7. **White.** The queen's pawn, two moves.
   **Black.** The king's bishop's pawn, one move.

8. **White.** The queen's bishop takes the pawn.
   **Black.** The queen's pawn, one move.

9. **White.** The queen's bishop's pawn, one move.
   **Black.** The pawn takes the knight.

10. **White.** The pawn retakes the pawn.
   **Black.** The king's knight, at home.

11. **White.** The queen, at her knight's third square.
   **Black.** The queen, at her king's second square.

12. **White.** The queen's knight, at his queen's second square.
   **Black.** The queen, at her king's bishop's square.

13. **White.** The king castles on his rook's side.
   **Black.** Loses the game.


---

*A back-game: a variation resuming its parent game at move 5.*

Source: Philidor, *Analysis of the Game of Chess* (London, P. Elmsley, 1777), collated with volume I of the edition of 1790.
