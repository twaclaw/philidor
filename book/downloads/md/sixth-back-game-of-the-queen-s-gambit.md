# Sixth Back-game of the Queen's Gambit

*At the 10th move of the White.*

10. **White.** The king's bishop's pawn takes the pawn.
   **Black.** The knight takes the king's pawn.

11. **White.** The knight retakes the knight.
   **Black.** The queen gives check. Ambo 12.

12. **White.** The knight, at his king's knight's third square.
   **Black.** The queen's bishop, at the adverse king's knight's fourth square.

13. **White.** The king's bishop, at his king's second square.
   **Black.** The queen takes the king's rook's pawn.

14. **White.** The king's rook, at its bishop's square.
   **Black.** The queen takes the knight, and gives check.

15. **White.** The king, at his queen's second square.
   **Black.** The queen's knight, at his queen's second square.

16. **White.** The rook takes the rook.
   **Black.** The rook retakes the rook.

17. **White.** The queen, at her king's square.
   **Black.** The rook, at the adverse king's bishop's second square, and wins the game. Liv ANALYSIS THE GAME CHESS. SUPPLEMENT THE EDITION OF 1749.


---

*A back-game: a variation resuming its parent game at move 10.*

Source: Philidor, *Analysis of the Game of Chess* (London, P. Elmsley, 1777), collated with volume I of the edition of 1790.
