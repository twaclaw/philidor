# First Back-game of the First Gambit

*On the 4th move of the White.*

4. **White.** The king's rook's pawn, two moves.
   **Black.** The king's knight's pawn, one move.

5. **White.** The king's knight, at the adversary's king's fourth square.
   **Black.** The king's rook's pawn, two moves.

6. **White.** The king's bishop, at his queen's bishop's fourth square.
   **Black.** The king's rook, at its second square.

7. **White.** The queen's pawn, two moves.
   **Black.** The queen's pawn, one move.

8. **White.** The king's knight, at his queen's third square.
   **Black.** The queen, at her king's second square.

9. **White.** The queen's knight, at his bishop's third square.
   **Black.** The king's knight, at his bishop's third square.

10. **White.** The queen, at her king's second square.
   **Black.** The king's pawn, one move; attacking the adversary's queen.

11. **White.** The king's knight's pawn takes the pawn.
   **Black.** The king's knight's pawn retakes the pawn.

12. **White.** The queen takes the pawn.
   **Black.** The queen's bishop, at the adverse king's knight's fourth square.

13. **White.** The queen, at her king's third square.
   **Black.** The king's bishop, at his rook's third square

14. **White.** The king's knight, at his king's bishop's fourth square.
   **Black.** The queen's bishop's pawn, one move.

15. **White.** The queen's bishop, at his queen's second square.
   **Black.** The king's bishop takes the knight.

16. **White.** The queen retakes the bishop.
   **Black.** The queen's pawn, one move.

17. **White.** The king's bishop, at his queen's third square.
   **Black.** The king's knight takes the king's pawn.

18. **White.** The knight, or the bishop, takes the knight.
   **Black.** The king's bishop's pawn, two moves.


---

*A back-game: a variation resuming its parent game at move 4.*

Source: Philidor, *Analysis of the Game of Chess* (London, P. Elmsley, 1777), collated with volume I of the edition of 1790.
