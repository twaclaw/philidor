# Method of giving Check-mate with a Bishop and a Knight

**Situation of the White**
- The king, at his fourth square.
- The king's bishop, at his square.
- The queen's knight, at his square. (a)
**Situation of the Black**
- The king, at his bishop's third square.

1. **White.** The bishop, at his queen's bishop's fourth square.
   **Black.** The king, at his knight's third square.

2. **White.** The king, at his bishop's fourth square.
   **Black.** The king, at his bishop's third square.

3. **White.** The knight, at his queen's bishop's third square.
   **Black.** The king, at his knight's third square.

4. **White.** The knight, at his king's fourth square.

   > The knight must endeavour to take away from the adverse king the opposite squares to the bishop's colour.

   **Black.** The king, at his rook's third square.

5. **White.** The king, at the adverse king's bishop's fourth square.
   **Black.** The king, at his rook's second square.

   > If he had played his king to his rook's fourth square, you must have given check with the bishop at your king's second square; and then, if he gets his king on the side of the corner of your bishop's colour, you will mate him in a few moves.

6. **White.** The king, at the adverse king's bishop's third square.
   **Black.** The king, at his rook's square.

   > If he had played his king to his rook's third square, you must have played your bishop to your king's second square, to oblige his king to get into the black corner, where your knight is very soon to force him out.

7. **White.** The knight, at the adverse queen's third square.
   **Black.** The king, at his rook's second square.

8. **White.** The knight, at the adverse king's bishop's second square.

   > In this position, the mate is forced in about eighteen or twenty moves,

   **Black.** The king, at his knight's square.

   > Your knight prevents his king from coming back into the corner. Now, the business of your knight must be, to cut his king off from the black squares, while your bishop will keep him from the white ones.

9. **White.** The bishop, at his queen's third square.
   **Black.** The king, at his bishop's square.

10. **White.** The bishop, at the adverse king's rook second square.
   **Black.** The king, at his home.

11. **White.** The knight, at the adverse king's fourth square.
   **Black.** The king, at his bishop's square.

   > As he might have played his king to his queen's square, I make a back-game of it. Riv

12. **White.** The knight gives check, at the adverse queen's second square.
   **Black.** The king, at his home.

13. **White.** The king, at the adverse king's third square.
   **Black.** The king, at his queen's square.

14. **White.** The king, at the adverse queen's third square.
   **Black.** The king, at his home.

15. **White.** The bishop gives check.
   **Black.** The king, at his queen's square.

16. **White.** The bishop, at the adverse king's bishop's second square.
   **Black.** The king, at his queen's bishop's square.

17. **White.** The knight, at the adverse queen's bishop's fourth square.
   **Black.** The king, at his queen's square.

18. **White.** The knight gives check, at the adverse queen's knight's second square.
   **Black.** The king, at his queen's bishop's square.

19. **White.** The king, at the adverse queen's bishop's third square.
   **Black.** The king, at his queen's knight's square.

20. **White.** The king, at the adverse queen's knight's third square.
   **Black.** The king, at his queen's bishop's square.

21. **White.** The bishop gives check.
   **Black.** The king, at his queen's knight's square.

22. **White.** The bishop, at the adverse queen's second square.
   **Black.** The king, at his queen's rook's square.

23. **White.** The knight, at the adverse queen's bishop's fourth square.
   **Black.** The king, at his queen's knight's square.

24. **White.** The knight gives check, at the adverse queen's rook's third square.
   **Black.** The king, at his rook's square.

25. **White.** The bishop gives check-mate.
