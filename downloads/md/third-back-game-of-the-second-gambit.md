# Third Back-game of the Second Gambit

*On the 8th move of the Black.*

8. **White.** The king, at his bishop's second square.
   **Black.** The king's knight, at his bishop's third square.

9. **White.** The queen, at her king's second square.
   **Black.** The bishop takes the knight.

10. **White.** The queen retakes the bishop.
   **Black.** The queen retakes the queen.

11. **White.** The pawn retakes the queen.
   **Black.** The king's bishop, at his knight's second square.

12. **White.** The king's rook's pawn, two moves.
   **Black.** The king's rook's pawn, one move.

13. **White.** The king's rook, at its knight's square.
   **Black.** The king's knight, at his rook's second square.

14. **White.** The queen's bishop takes the gambit's pawn.
   **Black.** The king's bishop takes the queen's pawn, and gives check.

15. **White.** The pawn takes the bishop.
   **Black.** The king's knight's pawn takes the bishop.

16. **White.** The king's rook, at the adverse king's knight's second square.
   **Black.** The queen's knight, at his bishop's third square.

17. **White.** The queen's knight, at his bishop's third square.
   **Black.** The queen's knight takes the pawn.

18. **White.** The bishop takes the pawn, and gives check.
   **Black.** The king, at his bishop's square.

19. **White.** The queen's rook, at its king's knight's square.
   **Black.** The queen's knight, at his bishop's third square.

20. **White.** The bishop, at his queen's knight's third square.
   **Black.** The queen's rook, at its queen's square.

21. **White.** The king's rook gives check, at the adverse king's bishop's second square.
   **Black.** The king, at his home.

22. **White.** The queen's rook, at the adverse king's knight's second square.
   **Black.** The king's knight, at his bishop's square.

23. **White.** The knight, at the adversary's queen's fourth square, and must very visibly win the game.


---

*A back-game: a variation resuming its parent game at move 8.*

Source: Philidor, *Analysis of the Game of Chess* (London, P. Elmsley, 1777), collated with volume I of the edition of 1790.
