# First Back-game of the Second Party

*Beginning from the 3d move of the Black.*

3. **White.** The queen's pawn, two steps.
   **Black.** The same.

4. **White.** The king's pawn takes the pawn.
   **Black.** The queen's bishop's pawn retakes the pawn.

5. **White.** The bishop gives check.
   **Black.** The bishop covers the check.

6. **White.** The bishop takes the bishop.
   **Black.** The knight retakes the bishop.

7. **White.** The queen's pawn takes the pawn.
   **Black.** The knight retakes the pawn.

8. **White.** The queen, at her king's second square.
   **Black.** The same.

9. **White.** The queen's knight, at his bishop's third square.
   **Black.** The king castles.

10. **White.** The bishop, at his king's bishop's fourth square.
   **Black.** The queen's knight, at his bishop's third square.

11. **White.** The king castles.
   **Black.** The queen takes the queen.

12. **White.** The king's knight retakes the queen.
   **Black.** The queen's pawn, one move.

13. **White.** The queen's knight, at his king's fourth square.
   **Black.** The king's bishop's pawn, one move.

14. **White.** The king's rook's pawn, two moves.
   **Black.** The king's rook's pawn, the same.

15. **White.** The king's rook, at its third square.
   **Black.** The king's knight, at his rook's third square.

16. **White.** The bishop takes the knight.
   **Black.** The rook retakes the bishop.

17. **White.** The king's rook, at its queen's third square.
   **Black.** The queen's rook, at its king's square.

18. **White.** The king's knight takes the pawn.
   **Black.** The knight, at the adverse queen's knight's fourth square.

19. **White.** The king's rook, at its king's third square.
   **Black.** The knight takes the rook's pawn, and gives check.

20. **White.** The king, at his queen's knight's square.
   **Black.** The knight retires.

21. **White.** The knight checks the king and rook at once, &c.


---

*A back-game: a variation resuming its parent game at move 3.*

Source: Philidor, *Analysis of the Game of Chess* (London, P. Elmsley, 1777), collated with volume I of the edition of 1790.
