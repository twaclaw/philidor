# Sequel to the First Back-game of Cunningham's Gambit

*At the 8th move of the Black.*

8. **White.** The king's bishop takes the pawn, and gives check.
   **Black.** The king, at his bishop's square.

9. **White.** The king's knight, at the adverse king's fourth square.
   **Black.** The king's knight, at his king's bishop's third square.

10. **White.** The king's bishop, at his queen's knight's third square.
   **Black.** The queen, at her king's square.

11. **White.** The king's knight, at the adverse king's bishop's second square.
   **Black.** The rook, at its knight's square.

12. **White.** The king's pawn, one move.
   **Black.** The queen's pawn, two moves.

13. **White.** The pawn takes the pawn.
   **Black.** The pawn retakes the pawn.

14. **White.** The bishop takes the pawn.
   **Black.** The queen's bishop, at the adverse king's knight's fourth square.

15. **White.** The queen, at her king's square.
   **Black.** The queen's bishop, at his king's rook's fourth square.

16. **White.** The queen's pawn, two moves.
   **Black.** The bishop takes the knight.

17. **White.** The queen's bishop gives check.
   **Black.** The rook covers the check.

18. **White.** The knight, at his queen's bishop's third square.
   **Black.** The bishop takes the bishop.

19. **White.** The knight retakes the bishop.
   **Black.** The queen, at her king's bishop's second square.

20. **White.** The knight takes the bishop.
   **Black.** The queen retakes the knight.

21. **White.** The queen takes the queen.
   **Black.** The king retakes the queen.

22. **White.** The bishop takes the rook, and, with the superiority of a rook, besides a good situation, will easily win the game.


---

*A back-game: a variation resuming its parent game at move 8.*

Source: Philidor, *Analysis of the Game of Chess* (London, P. Elmsley, 1777), collated with volume I of the edition of 1790.
