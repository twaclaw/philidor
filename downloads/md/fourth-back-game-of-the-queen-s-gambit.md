# Fourth Back-game of the Queen's Gambit

*At the 7th move of the White.*

7. **White.** The king's bishop takes the gambit's pawn.
   **Black.** The king's bishop's pawn takes the pawn.

8. **White.** The king's bishop's pawn retakes the pawn.
   **Black.** The king's knight, at the adverse king's knight's fourth square.

9. **White.** The king's knight, at his rook's third square.
   **Black.** The queen gives check.

10. **White.** The king, at his queen's second square.
   **Black.** The king's knight, at the adverse king's third square.

11. **White.** The queen, at her king's second square.
   **Black.** The queen's bishop, at the adverse king's knight's fourth square.

12. **White.** The queen, at her third square.
   **Black.** The king's knight takes the pawn.

13. **White.** The king's knight, at his square.
   **Black.** The queen, at the adverse king's square, giving check.

14. **White.** The king retires.
   **Black.** The king's bishop takes the knight, and must afterwards win the game.


---

*A back-game: a variation resuming its parent game at move 7.*

Source: Philidor, *Analysis of the Game of Chess* (London, P. Elmsley, 1777), collated with volume I of the edition of 1790.
