# First Back-game of the Third Gambit

   **Black.** The queen's knight, at his bishop's third square.

   **Black.** The king's bishop, at his king's second square.

   **Black.** The king's pawn takes the queen's pawn.

   **Black.** The king castles.

   **Black.** The queen's knight, at the adverse queen's bishop's fourth square.

   **Black.** The queen's knight, at the adverse queen's bishop's fourth square.

   **Black.** The queen gives check.

   **Black.** The queen, at the adverse queen's rook's third square.
