# Fourth Back-game of the Second Gambit

*Beginning at the 11th move of the Black.*

11. **White.** The queen takes the bishop.
   **Black.** The king's knight gives check, at the adverse king's knight's fourth square.

12. **White.** The king, at his knight's square.
   **Black.** The king's knight's pawn takes the pawn.

13. **White.** The queen's bishop takes the pawn.
   **Black.** The king's knight, at his bishop's third square.

14. **White.** The knight, at his queen's rook's third square.
   **Black.** The queen takes the queen.

15. **White.** The pawn retakes the queen.
   **Black.** The king's knight, at his rook's fourth square.

16. **White.** The king's rook takes the pawn.
   **Black.** The king's knight takes the bishop.

17. **White.** The rook retakes the knight.
   **Black.** The king's bishop's pawn, one move.

18. **White.** The king, at his bishop's second square.
   **Black.** The king castles.

19. **White.** The bishop, at the adversary's king's third square.
   **Black.** The bishop, at his king's second square.

20. **White.** The queen's rook, at its king's rook's square.
   **Black.** The king, at his queen's knight's square.

21. **White.** The bishop takes the knight.
   **Black.** The rook retakes the bishop.

22. **White.** The queen's rook, at the adverse king's rook's third square.
   **Black.** The queen's knight's pawn, one move.

23. **White.** The king's rook, at the adverse king's bishop's fourth square.
   **Black.** The bishop, at his queen's square.

24. **White.** The king's rook, at the adverse king's rook's fourth square.
   **Black.** The king, at his queen's knight's second square.

25. **White.** The king's bishop's pawn, one move.
   **Black.** The queen's bishop's pawn, one move.

26. **White.** The king's bishop's pawn, one move, and wins the game.


---

*A back-game: a variation resuming its parent game at move 11.*

Source: Philidor, *Analysis of the Game of Chess* (London, P. Elmsley, 1777), collated with volume I of the edition of 1790.
