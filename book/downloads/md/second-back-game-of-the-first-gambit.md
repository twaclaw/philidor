# Second Back-game of the First Gambit

*From the 4th move of the Black.*

4. **White.** The king's bishop, at his queen's bishop's fourth square.
   **Black.** The king's knight's pawn, one move.

5. **White.** The king's knight, at the adversary's king's fourth square.
   **Black.** The queen gives check.

6. **White.** The king, at his bishop's square.
   **Black.** The king's knight, at his rook's third square.

7. **White.** The queen's pawn, two moves.
   **Black.** The queen's pawn, one move.

8. **White.** The king's knight, at his queen's third square.
   **Black.** The king's pawn, one move.

9. **White.** The king's knight's pawn, one move.
   **Black.** The queen gives check.

10. **White.** The king, at his bishop's second square.
   **Black.** The queen gives check.

11. **White.** The king, at his third square.
   **Black.** The king's knight, at his square.

12. **White.** The king's knight, at his king's bishop's fourth square.
   **Black.** The king's bishop, at his rook's third square.

13. **White.** The king's bishop, at his home, attacking the queen, and taking her by force.
   **Black.** The queen takes the rook, not knowing what to do better.

14. **White.** The king's bishop gives check, and afterwards takes the queen.

*A back-game: a variation resuming its parent game at move 4.*
