# Second Back-game of the Third Gambit

*Beginning at the 3d move of the Black.*

3. **White.** The king's pawn takes the adverse queen's pawn.
   **Black.** The king's pawn takes the bishop's pawn.

4. **White.** The king's knight, at his bishop's third square.
   **Black.** The queen takes the pawn.

5. **White.** The queen's pawn, two moves.
   **Black.** The queen gives check, at the adverse king's fourth square.

6. **White.** The king, at his bishop's second square.
   **Black.** The king's bishop, at his king's second square.

7. **White.** The king's bishop, at his queen's third square.
   **Black.** The queen, at her queen's bishop's third square.

8. **White.** The queen's bishop takes the pawn.
   **Black.** The queen's bishop, at his king's third square.

9. **White.** The queen, at her king's second square.
   **Black.** The queen, at her second square.

10. **White.** The queen's bishop's pawn, two moves.
   **Black.** The queen's bishop's pawn, one move.

11. **White.** The queen's knight, at his bishop's third square.
   **Black.** The king's knight, at his bishop's third square.

12. **White.** The king's rook's pawn, one move.
   **Black.** The king castles.

13. **White.** The king's knight's pawn, two moves.
   **Black.** The king's bishop, at his queen's third square.

14. **White.** The king's knight, at the adverse king's fourth square.
   **Black.** The bishop takes the knight.

15. **White.** The pawn retakes the bishop.
   **Black.** The king's knight, at his king's square.

16. **White.** The queen's rook, at its queen's square.
   **Black.** The queen, at her king's second square.

17. **White.** The king's knight's pawn, one move.
   **Black.** The queen's knight, at his queen's second square.

18. **White.** The queen, at the adverse king's rook's fourth square.
   **Black.** The king's knight's pawn, one move.

19. **White.** The queen, at the adverse king's rook's third square.
   **Black.** The queen gives check.

20. **White.** The king, at his knight's third square.
   **Black.** The queen's knight takes the adversary's king's pawn.

21. **White.** The knight, at his king's fourth square.
   **Black.** The queen, at the adversary's queen's fourth square.

22. **White.** The knight gives check, at the adverse king's bishop's third square.
   **Black.** The knight takes the knight.

23. **White.** The pawn retakes the knight.
   **Black.** lost, the mate being forced.
