# Fifth Back-game of the First Gambit

*From the 7th move of the Black.*

7. **White.** The queen's bishop's pawn, one move.
   **Black.** The queen's bishop, at the adverse king's knight's square.

8. **White.** The queen, at her knight's third square.
   **Black.** The queen's bishop, at his king's rook's fourth square.

9. **White.** The king's rook's pawn takes the pawn.
   **Black.** The rook's pawn retakes the pawn.

10. **White.** The king's rook takes the bishop.
   **Black.** The rook retakes the rook.

11. **White.** The king's bishop takes the pawn, and, giving check to the king and rook, wins a piece, and consequently the game.


---

*A back-game: a variation resuming its parent game at move 7.*

Source: Philidor, *Analysis of the Game of Chess* (London, P. Elmsley, 1777), collated with volume I of the edition of 1790.
