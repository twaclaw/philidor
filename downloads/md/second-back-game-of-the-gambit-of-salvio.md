# Second Back-game of the Gambit of Salvio

*On the 7th move of the White.*

7. **White.** The queen's pawn, two moves.
   **Black.** The queen's pawn, one move.

   > If he had taken your king's pawn, you should have played your queen to your king's second square.

8. **White.** The king's knight, at his queen's third square.
   **Black.** The king's, or gambit's pawn, one move.

9. **White.** The queen's knight, at his king's third square.
   **Black.** The king's, or gambit's pawn, takes the pawn.

10. **White.** The king retakes the pawn.
   **Black.** The queen gives check, &c.

   > It is needless to go farther; you get again into the same moves of the variable of the first back-game, with this difference, that you have one pawn less.


---

*A back-game: a variation resuming its parent game at move 7.*

Source: Philidor, *Analysis of the Game of Chess* (London, P. Elmsley, 1777), collated with volume I of the edition of 1790.
