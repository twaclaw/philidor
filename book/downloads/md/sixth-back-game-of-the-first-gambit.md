# Sixth Back-game of the First Gambit

*From the 7th move of the Black.*

7. **White.** The queen's bishop's pawn, one move.
   **Black.** The queen's bishop, at his king's third square.

8. **White.** The king's bishop takes the bishop.
   **Black.** The pawn retakes the bishop.

9. **White.** The queen, at her knight's third square.
   **Black.** The queen, at her bishop's square, to defend the two attacked pawns.

10. **White.** The king's rook's pawn takes the pawn.
   **Black.** The rook's pawn retakes the pawn.

11. **White.** The king's rook takes the rook.
   **Black.** The bishop takes the rook.

12. **White.** The king's knight takes the pawn.
   **Black.** The king, at his second square.

13. **White.** The queen's bishop takes the pawn.
   **Black.** The queen's knight, at his bishop's third square.

14. **White.** The queen's knight, at his queen's second square.
   **Black.** The queen's rook's pawn, two moves.

15. **White.** The king castles.
   **Black.** The queen's knight's pawn, two moves.

16. **White.** The rook, at its king's rook's square.
   **Black.** The king's knight, at his bishop's third square.

17. **White.** The rook takes the bishop.
   **Black.** The queen retakes the rook.

18. **White.** The queen takes the king's pawn, and gives check.
   **Black.** The king retires where he pleases, standing very badly on any square where he may go.

*A back-game: a variation resuming its parent game at move 7.*
